§ 06

Questions

Is this a diagnosis?

No. A physician reviews every result, and you'll get a PDF to share with your own provider.

When should I take the first kit?

About six weeks after giving birth.

Can I buy just one kit?

Yes. The Series of 3 is the better deal and shows you a trend.

The Hera Postpartum Panel is not a substitute for medical care. If you have symptoms that worry you, contact your provider. In an emergency, call 911.
