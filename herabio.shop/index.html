Sakuya Matcha is made with real matcha, so it contains some natural caffeine, roughly what you'd get from a cup of green tea. The exact amount per scoop will be listed here after lab testing.

FAQs

Can I drink matcha while pregnant?
Matcha contains caffeine, so most guidance says yes, in moderation. See the caffeine section above, and check with your doctor.

Does it contain iron?
Yes. Iron is part of the formula, in a gentle chelated form chosen to absorb well even when mixed with milk.

Is it dairy-free or vegan?
The powder itself contains no dairy, and you choose the milk. Full allergen and dietary details will be posted once the formula is final.

Can I take it with my current prenatal?
Sakuya Matcha is a complete prenatal, made to replace your current one rather than add to it. Ask your doctor before combining supplements.

What does it taste like?
A creamy matcha latte: ceremonial-grade matcha, lightly sweetened with monk fruit, rounded out with lucuma and a pinch of sea salt.
