PLANNING

Why Hera

Hera was queen of Olympus: goddess of marriage, of birth, of every stage of a woman's life, and a fierce protector of what was hers. She is usually shown with a pomegranate, the old sign of fertility and abundance. We took the name because that is the standard: sovereignty over your own biology, and nothing less than the best for it.

XX Biosciences

Science built for bodies with two X chromosomes, rather than adapted to them after the fact. Hera builds for women, XX men and nonbinary people: anyone whose body runs on XX biology. We write for the need, not the label, so you will see pregnancy, cycles, testosterone and menopause named here, and you addressed as you.

Why biotech

For most of modern medicine, the XX body was studied by studying men and scaling down. The result is dosing that was never tested on us, supplements that cannot be absorbed, whole stages of life that get no real research, and products that talk down to the people who buy them. Half of humanity is not a niche. So we run the trials nobody ran, and we publish the proof: third-party test results for every lot, and the design of every study we run, so you never have to take our word for it.

How we work

We study the XX body on its own terms. No stage is minor. Every seed counts: many small, exacting things add up to a life. Our first product is a prenatal because that is where the founder, a designer in Brooklyn, found the gap first. The strands that follow reach the rest.
